Adjunctions

SEP 08, 2026

Suppose a real world problem PPP is solved by some theory TTT by Vassili. Vassili is well known for his work on TTT and this particular problem PPP is such that you can see it as a pratical isomorphism of TTT, once you known TTT well enough.

Is it possible to find another theory UUU that is also a solution to PPP? Such UUU should be different enough from TTT as to mask its connection with Vassili, which we are trying to preserve.

We don’t care about any generality here at all. It suffices to show some specific examples of TTT, PPP, and UUU.

Definition

Formally, an adjunction between functors F⊣GF \dashv GF⊣G gives a natural bijection

HomD(FX,Y)≅HomC(X,GY)\mathrm{Hom}_D(FX, Y) \cong \mathrm{Hom}_C(X, GY)HomD​(FX,Y)≅HomC​(X,GY)

for all XXX in CCC and YYY in DDD. Masking Vassili’s fingerprints on TTT amounts to finding a UUU related to TTT by such an adjunction, rather than an outright isomorphism.
